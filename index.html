M.Tech Thesis · IIT Kanpur

AI-Physics hybrid modelling of land–atmosphere interactions

Evaporative water stress matters for irrigation and drought monitoring, yet the flux towers that measure it are sparse. My thesis explores whether widely observed daily temperature swings can stand in for those measurements, by combining physical process knowledge with machine learning.
