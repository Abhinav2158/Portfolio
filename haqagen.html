HAQAGen

NIR-to-RGB spectral image colorization

Overview

Near-infrared cameras capture scenes that visible-light cameras cannot, but their images carry no colour. HAQAGen is a NIR-to-RGB colorization framework that targets two weaknesses of earlier models: globally consistent colour and fine texture at native resolution. The paper, “Histogram Assisted Quality Aware Generative Model for Resolution Invariant NIR Image Colorization”, was accepted at the IEEE/CVF Winter Conference on Applications of Computer Vision (WACV) 2026.

01

Architecture

Extends a Mamba-based (ColorMamba) backbone with a dual-branch RGB–HSV generator and SPADE-conditioned decoding for improved local chromatic consistency.

02

Training objective

A composite objective combining a differentiable histogram (CDF) loss, a VGG-19 perceptual loss and frozen-autoencoder texture supervision — enforcing global chromatic consistency while preserving fine-grained textures.

03

Inference

A resolution-invariant sliding-window pipeline with Hanning blending for seam-free processing at native resolution.
