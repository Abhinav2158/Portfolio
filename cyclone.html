Cyclone Track Prediction

Multi-horizon trajectory forecasting

Overview

A multi-horizon cyclone trajectory forecasting system that predicts cyclone positions at 6–72 h lead times from 149 physics-informed spatio-temporal features and coordinate-displacement targets.

11.09 kmRMSE at 6 h
60.87 kmMAE at 24 h
40.9%RMSE reduction vs Ridge (up to)
149engineered features
01

Feature engineering

Kinematic, intensity, rotational, lagged and multi-scale temporal features — including Coriolis dynamics, angular momentum, velocity trends, pressure gradients and distance-to-land.

02

Modelling

Seven ML models plus ensemble learning, benchmarked under a strict 1980–2019 train / 2020–2025 test split.

03

Result

Up to 40.9% lower RMSE than a Ridge baseline.
