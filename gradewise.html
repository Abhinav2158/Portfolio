Gradewise AI

Intelligent automated grading system

Overview

Gradewise AI grades written answers the way a careful examiner would: by pointing to the evidence. Each question is decomposed into atomic rubrics, and several models must agree on the exact span of the answer that satisfies each one.

0.8620Quadratic Weighted Kappa
3models in consensus
01

Evidence extraction

Combines an LLM, DeBERTa-v3 and Sentence-MPNet for consensus-based, character-level span extraction against each atomic rubric.

02

Confidence-gated scoring

A scoring heuristic built on span Jaccard overlap, score variance and lexical grounding decides how much to trust each automated grade.

03

Human-in-the-loop

Submissions the system is uncertain about are routed automatically to a human reviewer.
