India Heatwave Forecasting

Spatiotemporal ConvLSTM

Overview

A spatiotemporal deep-learning model for 5-day heatwave forecasting across India, trained on gridded meteorological and radiative data and evaluated both as a temperature forecast and as a heatwave detector.

1.20 °CRMSE
0.954R²
0.652POD
5 daysforecast horizon
01

Model and data

A ConvLSTM over 12 meteorological and radiative channels on a 33 × 31 grid, with leakage-safe P95 labelling and a heatwave-weighted MSE loss.

02

Evaluation

Forecast skill and heatwave detection measured with RMSE, MAE, R², CSI, POD and FAR, including lead-time degradation.

03

Interpretability

Occlusion-based explainability to see which input channels and regions drive the forecast.
